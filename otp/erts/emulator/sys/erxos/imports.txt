# What ERTS's objects may import from outside themselves. Read by
# make check-runtime-seam and the native build; the rule is in
# docs/design-runtime.md, "What ERTS imports". Names are separated by white space;
# a section is a group that can be dropped whole.
#
# The sections up to [musl] are the list the design keeps. Every [stock_*]
# section is the stock Unix layer sys/erxos was copied from. Its comment says what
# empties it, and that change deletes the section. The check reports a listed name
# that nothing imports.
