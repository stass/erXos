# What ERTS's objects may import from outside themselves and liberx. Read by
# make check-runtime-seam; the rule is in docs/design-runtime.md, "What ERTS
# imports". Names are separated by white space; a section is a group that can
# be dropped whole.
#
# The sections up to [libsystem] are the list the design keeps. Every [stock_*]
# section is the stock Unix layer sys/erxos was copied from. Its comment says what
# empties it, and that change deletes the section. The check reports a listed name
# that nothing imports.

# Emptied by edits 30 and 32, by time over erx_clock_ns, by the deleted erl_poll.c

# Emptied when a release booting from its archive deletes stock's unix_prim_file.c
# and the stage that keeps it linked: nothing else imports these.

# What else reaches descriptors and directories: the drivers, ports and forker, the
# poll set's wake pipes, prim_tty, the socket NIFs, the crash dump and sys.c. Each

[stock_descriptors]
