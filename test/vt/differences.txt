# The differences of erx_vt from its oracle, libvterm 0.3.3, over test/vt/corpus.rb
# (docs/design-display.md, "The oracle"), read by test/host/erx_vt_oracle_test.rb.
# Each entry is a corpus case: its kind, documented (erx_vt's behaviour stands, as the
# reason says) or upstream (a libvterm bug, the reference named), the reason, and
# erx_vt's dump of the case, which erx_vt must give exactly. A case on which the two
# terminals differ and that no entry lists fails the test, and so does an entry on
# which they agree.
