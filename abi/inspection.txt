# The inspection schema (docs/design-abi.md, "Inspection schema"): units,
# value types, stopgap reporters, kinds with their attributes in field
# order, and paths. Its members have one namespace of their own; a root
# limit, scalar, enum or flags is named by @name. tools/gen_abi.rb
# generates erx_schema and the host tool's table from it.
