# Rows from Linux carry facts only, a device's identifiers and that it needs a

# comment, name or ordering of that GPL-2.0 file is reproduced here.
#
# Lines are tokens separated by spaces, # starting a comment:
#
#   source NAME RELEASE COMMIT FORM    a source rows may cite, at its pinned release
#   quirk VVVV:PPPP LLLL-HHHH KIND... from CITE...
#
# A source's COMMIT is 12 to 40 lowercase hexadecimal digits, and its FORM how rows cite
# it: line, as NAME:LINE, the line of the source a row comes from, or bare, as NAME
# alone. A quirk row names a device by its vendor and product identifiers, four
# lowercase hexadecimal digits each, or * for every product of the vendor, and the
# inclusive range of its bcdDevice, four digits each; its kinds, one or more members of
# the enum usb_quirk of abi/formats/usb.txt, each once; and one or more citations of
# declared sources. Rows are sorted by vendor, product (* first), then range, and no
# two share all three; a device takes every kind of every row it matches.
#
