# The block transport contract's bounds (docs/design-storage.md, "Data model"), erXos's
# choice, which every block transport keeps: virtio-blk's and the SD host controller's
# device processes and the USB Bulk-Only transport's core. A request still waiting
# blk_wait_limit_ms after its acceptance is answered {device, deadline} without being
# issued; one issued blk_in_flight_ms ago and not complete is a failure that ends its
# device process, or for a USB unit its medium. Their sum stays below erx_blk's 30 s call
# timeout, so the transport answers before its caller gives up.
limit blk_wait_limit_ms u32 10000
limit blk_in_flight_ms u32 18000
# The most sectors one SD host controller request moves, 32 KiB: the most whose write a
# card within its specification finishes inside blk_in_flight_ms (63 blocks at 250 ms of
# busy and the last at 500 ms). It is the SD transport's own limit; virtio-blk's follows
# from its pool buffer and USB's from the xHCI bulk buffer.
limit blk_sd_max_sectors u32 64
