# A session's line to the SSH domain (docs/design-system.md, "A session's lines";
# docs/design-ssh.md, "Sessions, ttys and shells"): a facet the session mints for the
# SSH domain when a login succeeds, which no manifest row grants. A channel process of
# the SSH domain calls open_tty through it, and the line answers. What the session
# sends a channel, the console messages (console.txt) and a tty's ending, exited or
# signaled as design-ssh.md's table gives them, comes from the tty's relay and is no
# operation of this interface. A job's open_job and its messages enter it with the
# answer to design-ssh.md's open question "How a job's messages reach their job".
# The grant's quantities (docs/design-system.md, "A session's lines"): max_request is
# the smallest power of two not below both measures of the largest request, an
# open_tty of {exec, Command} with a 32 KiB command and a 64-byte TERM, its caller's
# pid and alias of a 255-character node name: erlang:external_size/1 of
# {'$gen_call', From, Request}, the envelope left out, and the term's heap words times
# the word size plus the bytes of the binaries it references, each about 33 KiB.
# in_flight is 4, since a channel's open_tty is answered at once.
service tty_line 12
  served_as message
  served_by session
  operation open_tty 1
  quantity max_request 65536
  quantity in_flight 4
  quantity reductions 100000
  quantity period_ns 1000000000
  quantity max_heap_size 65536
# The signals a tty's ending {signaled, Signal} names are abi/signals.txt's signal set
# tty_master, each by its name with sig before it, as a program's end names it
# (docs/design-programs.md, "A program's end"); the channel sends it as SSH's
# exit-signal, by the name without sig in capitals.
# The line's bounds (docs/design-ssh.md, "Sessions, ttys and shells" and "Limits and who
# pays"): a connection holds at most tty_line_max_channels channels open, FreeBSD's
# MaxSessions, and the line answers limited past them; an exec's command is 1 to
# tty_line_command_max bytes, and a job_input or job_output carries at most tty_line_data_max bytes,
# the channel's packet size. A pty-req's TERM is at most tty_term_max bytes, terminfo's
# names being short, and its rows and columns each at most console_side_max (console.txt),
# a side of zero taken as the default; the session's own first stage, erx_ssh_dev_channel,
# takes a pty-req under the same bounds.
limit tty_line_max_channels u32 10
limit tty_line_command_max u32 32768
limit tty_line_data_max u32 32768
limit tty_term_max u32 64
