# The log interface (docs/design-log.md, "The log facet"): a domain's records for the
# system log, served by the system domain. write takes a list of at most log_write_max
# records {Level, Text}, each level one of the logger's eight and each text a binary of
# at most log_write_text_max bytes, and answers ok once the service has taken the batch.
# erx_log_facet:decode/1 refuses anything else with {error, badarg}; the facet answers
# {error, busy} past its limit in flight, {error, unavailable} with no service and
# {error, failed} when the service ends before it answers, and drops a request past its
# max_request unanswered (erx_facet). The grantee, its principal and its name are the
# service's, from the facet, never the request's, so a domain can lie only in its own
# text. log_write_text_max is the service's bound on one record's text, distinct from
# and larger than the kernel ring's log_text_max (2112 bytes).
service log 9
  operation write 1
limit log_write_max u32 16
limit log_write_text_max u32 4096
