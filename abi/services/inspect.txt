# The inspect interface (docs/design-live.md, "The inspect service"): the inspector's two
# queries from another domain, served by the system domain. inspect takes a path and
# options and answers {ok, Record} or {error, Reason}; entries takes a path and answers
# the records of a listing's entries, {ok, Records} or {partial, Records}.
# inspect_reply_max bounds an answer's encoding in bytes: the inspector cuts a listing or
# an append-only record's entries before it, and the generator refuses a schema whose
# record without entries passes it. Every grantee of an inspect facet and the system
# domain, its server, carry the three floors the funnel applies to a frame (property 16;
# docs/design-domains.md, "Floors from a protocol"): inspect_max_frame_min bytes of
# max_frame, an answer and its envelope in one frame; inspect_message_heap_min words of
# max_message_heap, four times the answer bound; and inspect_funnel_depth_min of
# funnel_depth, which the generator computes from abi/inspection.txt
# (tools/lib/erx/abi_inspection_bounds.rb). A query worker's max_heap_size is
# inspect_worker_heap words, it lives at most inspect_query_max milliseconds, and it waits
# inspect_agent_wait milliseconds for one domain's runtime agent, as long as it waits for
# the domain service's view of the domains it may ask. The
# sizes are provisional, for Phase 1's trusted workloads, until the measurement of
# docs/todo-performance.md sets them. A word is 8 bytes, as manifest max_message_heap
# and OTP's max_heap_size count it.
service inspect 7
  operation inspect 1
  operation entries 2
limit inspect_reply_max u32 262144
limit inspect_max_frame_min u32 524288
limit inspect_message_heap_min u32 131072
limit inspect_worker_heap u32 2097152
limit inspect_query_max u32 5000
limit inspect_agent_wait u32 500
