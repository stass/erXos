# The network domain's interfaces (docs/design-network-stack.md, "The grant"): net,
# a client's sockets, and netadmin, interface configuration and every client's
# statistics. Their operations are the socket protocol's own messages, which no
# facet row names, so neither declares any. The manifest grants neither to the
# system domain (property 15), and every grantee of net carries frames of at least
# net_max_frame_min bytes, the protocol's max_frame floor: a piece of 64 KiB and its
# envelope (docs/design-domains.md, "Floors from a protocol";
# docs/design-network-stack.md, "Message sizes").
service net 3
service netadmin 4
limit net_max_frame_min u32 131072
