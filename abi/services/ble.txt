# The ble interface (docs/design-bluetooth.md, "The `ble` facet"): what an app in another
# domain asks of the Bluetooth domain's GATT registry for the services its grant names.
# attach makes the caller a service's handler; set gives a characteristic's value for
# reads; notify and indicate send a value on a link; reply answers a write handed to the
# handler. A facet row says operations none and the facet's admit/3 decides what passes.
# services names the GATT services the grantee handles, at most 8 names of abi/gatt, which
# the packer sorts into strict byte order. No handler hears a bonded link's identity
# address. ble_grant is its grant checker: the service of this interface is named ble and
# served by a configured domain, no grant goes to the system domain, and every name is a
# service abi/gatt declares. Its
# quantities are those of the app template's row the build derives when the apps row
# names ble (docs/design-packaging.md, "The apps row"): a request of at most 1,024
# octets, above the largest a handler makes, a set or notify of the largest value
# abi/gatt declares, 244 octets, with its term's framing; 16 in flight, a handler's
# notifies to its links and its answers to their writes; the reductions and period
# every interface takes; and a heap of 65,536 words, room for 16 requests of 1,024
# octets many times over.
service ble 20
  served_as message
  operation attach 1
  operation set 2
  operation notify 3
  operation indicate 4
  operation reply 5
  parameter services 1 identifier set 8 bytes 63 checked_by ble_grant
  quantity max_request 1024
  quantity in_flight 16
  quantity reductions 100000
  quantity period_ns 1000000000
  quantity max_heap_size 65536
