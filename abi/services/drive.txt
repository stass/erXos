# A drive's grant to the media domain (docs/design-drives.md, "How the grant reaches the
# media domain"; docs/design-system.md, "A drive window as a grant"): a facet the drive's
# serving domain, the domain whose units hold the drive's device, mints for the media
# domain, which no service or facet row declares: each drive window row of the manifest is
# one grant of it, installed in the media domain's registry as {drive, N}, N the row's
# drive number. Its one operation, window, answers {ok, Proxy}, a window proxy that takes
# the medium's read, write, flush and info under the grant's limits, or
# {error, no_medium} while the drive holds none.
# The grant's quantities (docs/design-drives.md gives each figure's reason): max_request
# is the smallest power of two not below the largest request the proxy takes, a write of
# a USB transport's max_sectors, 32 KiB, in its call's envelope; in_flight is
# one for each of a medium's volumes and one for its raw process, each of which sends one
# request at a time; reductions and
# period_ns are the share of the serving domain's grant the media domain's requests may
# spend; max_heap_size, 1 MiB on a 64-bit runtime, holds in_flight requests of
# max_request and their replies, and the facet and its proxy each run with it.
service drive 23
  served_as message
  served_by drive
  operation window 1
  quantity max_request 65536
  quantity in_flight 9
  quantity reductions 1000000
  quantity period_ns 1000000000
  quantity max_heap_size 131072
